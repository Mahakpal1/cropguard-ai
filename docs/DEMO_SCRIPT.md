# Five-minute demo script

Prepare: 3 lab photos, 3 messy field/phone photos, 1 photo of an unsupported crop (e.g. apple), 1 blurry photo. Record a backup video first.

1. **(30 s)** Say what it is and the one-line pipeline. Point at the header chip: is the model *live* or *simulated*? Be upfront.
2. **(60 s)** Upload a lab photo → watch the scanner and the four steps → diagnosis, confidence ring, severity (call it an estimate), advice tabs.
3. **(45 s)** "How the AI works": open the graph and the four panels (input channels, colour analysis, feature filters, class probabilities with the confidence gate).
4. **(45 s)** Upload a messy field photo → show the honest outcome (lower confidence or "please retake"). Upload the apple leaf → "unsupported crop".
5. **(30 s)** Switch to हिन्दी → Read aloud.
6. **(45 s)** History and overview cards (real saved scans).
7. **(45 s)** Evaluation slide: lab accuracy vs field accuracy, calibration plot, what you did to narrow the gap. End with limitations and next steps.
